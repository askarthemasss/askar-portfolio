the person behind the commits

My name is Mohamed Askar. I am a frontend software engineer based in Chennai, currently building enterprise applications at Agilysys. I have over four and a half years of experience, with a deep focus on Angular and TypeScript. My work centers on clean architecture, performance, thoughtful state management (RxJS, NgRx), and large-scale migrations — including navigating a full AngularJS to Angular 16 rewrite for a global production platform.

Beyond my core frontend expertise, I am actively expanding into AI engineering (RAG, agents, MCP integrations) and backend architecture (.NET Core, C#). I am drawn to building resilient systems for people who have high standards, with a long-term focus on software architecture and bootstrapped B2B SaaS products.

a mirror made of questions

LifeOps

A personal productivity Progressive Web App built with Angular, TypeScript, and Supabase. Centered on a Timeline feature — the idea that life is better understood as a flow of events than a list of tasks. It includes a custom "Obsidian Flow" dark theme designed for it, a thoughtful Supabase schema, and a modular prompting approach used during development. This is a living personal tool, not a product demo.

an attempt to give time a shape I can hold

Kairos & Recurra

Active personal projects currently under construction — part of a broader practice of shipping small, real things rather than planning perfect things. Kairos explores time and task in one direction; Recurra examines recurring patterns in another. Both are vehicles for learning by making.

two instruments still being tuned

Bus Profitability Calculator

A React-based tool built during an actual Chidambaram–Chennai bus journey. The idea came from watching the road and thinking about the economics of the bus I was sitting in — fuel costs, ticket revenue, maintenance margins. Built under constraint, in motion, with limited connectivity. A small exercise in turning observation into working software.

a tool born on a moving bus
