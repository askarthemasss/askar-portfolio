the person behind the commits

My name is Mohamed Askar. I am a frontend software engineer based in Chennai, currently building enterprise applications at Agilysys. I have over four and a half years of experience, with a deep focus on Angular and TypeScript. My work centers on clean architecture, performance, thoughtful state management (RxJS, NgRx), and large-scale migrations — including navigating a full AngularJS to Angular 16 rewrite for a global production platform.

Beyond my core frontend expertise, I am actively expanding into AI engineering (RAG, agents, MCP integrations) and backend architecture (.NET Core, C#). I am drawn to building resilient systems for people who have high standards, with a long-term focus on software architecture and bootstrapped B2B SaaS products.

places the line has passed through

Sept 2021 — early period

Zappyworks

Intern → Full-time Software Developer

The transition from intern to full-time came after roughly four months — fast enough to feel earned, slow enough to learn properly. This was where I first understood what shipping meant: building inside Zoho Creator applications, constructing Zoho CRM lead conversion flows, integrating with Zoho Books for procurement and financial workflows.

I built purchase-order and returns tracking systems, wrote DOM-heavy JavaScript pages, consumed and produced REST APIs. Each feature was a small lesson in how enterprise software actually works — not in theory, but in the daily discipline of handling edge cases.

Received the Super Squad Award for collaborative work on a particularly complex web page — the kind of recognition that matters because it comes from the people building alongside you.

3+ years · ongoing

Agilysys

Software Developer — Hospitality Enterprise

The hospitality domain teaches you things no tutorial can: multi-property configurations, fiscal calculations that must remain correct across currencies and timezones, reliability expectations that come from real hotels in real countries running real operations. Previous work on the STAY product, current work on VERSA.

Specific contributions that mark the path: SSO implementation, ESLint cleanup and enforcement across the codebase, meaningful Jest test contributions that raised reliability, fiscal and billing bug fixing across international properties, creation of a reusable app-ag-billing-currency wrapper component that standardized how the entire application handles currency display.

The largest undertaking: the AngularJS to Angular 16 migration — not a weekend rewrite but a sustained, careful effort on production hospitality applications used across international properties. I know what it means to keep the system running while rebuilding it underneath.

LifeOps

A personal productivity Progressive Web App built with Angular, TypeScript, and Supabase. Centered on a Timeline feature — the idea that life is better understood as a flow of events than a list of tasks. It includes a custom "Obsidian Flow" dark theme designed for it, a thoughtful Supabase schema, and a modular prompting approach used during development. This is a living personal tool, not a product demo.

Bus Profitability Calculator

A React-based tool built during an actual Chidambaram–Chennai bus journey. The idea came from watching the road and thinking about the economics of the bus I was sitting in — fuel costs, ticket revenue, maintenance margins. Built under constraint, in motion, with limited connectivity. A small exercise in turning observation into working software.
